## Sourcing note

Both entries directly cite `praha.eu` (the city's own official
domain, for the current governing law) and Wikipedia (for the 1784
historical unification of the four cities). A date discrepancy
between sources for the current law (17 May 2000 vs 13 April 2000)
was resolved in favor of the directly-read primary source (`praha.eu`'s
own hosted document), which shows 13 April 2000.

## Scope

A **read-only reference/archive** catalog — not an Advisor⊣Governor
actuation actor. It proposes or executes nothing on the Capital City
of Prague's behalf.

Coverage is reported honestly (see `ordinance.facts/coverage`): a
municipality not in `catalog` has **no spec-basis**, full stop — never
fabricate one.

## Data

- `src/ordinance/facts.cljc` — the catalog, source of truth.
- `schema/ordinance.edn` — DataScript schema.
- `data/datascript-tx.edn` — derived DataScript tx-data (query this
  alongside other `cloud-itonami`/`etzhayyim` compliance-fact sources via
  `com-junkawasaki/root`'s `scripts/compliance-fact-query.cljs`).

Both entries directly confirmed: the **Act on the Capital City of
Prague** (Zákon č. 131/2000 Sb., 13 April 2000) and the **1784
unification** of the four historic cities of Prague under Emperor
Joseph II.
